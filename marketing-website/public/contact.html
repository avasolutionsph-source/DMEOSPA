Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need to make an appointment?

Walk-ins are welcome, but we recommend booking in advance especially during peak hours and weekends to ensure availability.

What should I expect during my first visit?

Our therapists will consult with you about any health conditions, preferences, and areas of focus before beginning your personalized treatment.

What are your pricing and payment options?

Prices start at ₱199/hour for Swedish massage. We accept cash and most major payment methods for your convenience.

What should I wear and bring?

Come in comfortable clothing. We provide all towels, robes, and premium oils. Just bring yourself and prepare to relax!
