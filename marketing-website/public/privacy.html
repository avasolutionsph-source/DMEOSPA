2. Personal Data We Collect

Account Information

  • Name, email address, phone number
  • Business name and address
  • Password (encrypted)
  • Subscription plan and payment information

Business Operations Data

  • Employee records (names, positions, contact details, attendance, payroll)
  • Customer information (names, contact details, service history)
  • Transaction records and sales data
  • Booking and appointment information
  • Inventory and product data

Technical Data

  • Device information and browser type
  • IP address and location data
  • Usage analytics and performance metrics
  • Error logs and diagnostic information

3. Legal Basis for Processing

  • Contract Performance: To provide our business management services
  • Legitimate Interest: To improve our services and prevent fraud
  • Consent: For marketing communications and analytics
  • Legal Obligation: For tax records and regulatory compliance

4. How We Use Your Data

  • Provide and maintain our business management platform
  • Process transactions and manage subscriptions
  • Send service updates and support communications
  • Improve our services through analytics (with consent)
  • Comply with legal obligations (BIR, DOLE, etc.)
  • Send marketing communications (with explicit consent)

5. Data Sharing and Transfers

Third-Party Processors

  • Hosting: Render.com (USA) - Data Processing Agreement in place
  • CDN: Netlify (USA) - Standard Contractual Clauses
  • Database: MongoDB Atlas (USA) - Adequate protection measures
  • Analytics: Only with your explicit consent

Cross-border transfers: We ensure adequate protection through Data Processing Agreements and Standard Contractual Clauses approved by the National Privacy Commission.

6. Your Rights Under the Data Privacy Act

You have the right to:

  • Access: Request a copy of your personal data
  • Rectification: Correct inaccurate or incomplete data
  • Erasure: Request deletion of your data
  • Portability: Receive your data in a machine-readable format
  • Object: Opt-out of processing for marketing purposes
  • Restrict: Limit how we process your data
  • Complaint: File a complaint with the National Privacy Commission

To exercise these rights, contact our DPO at dpo@avasolutionsph.com or use the data management tools in your account settings.

7. Data Retention

  • Account Data: Until account deletion + 30 days
  • Transaction Records: 5 years (BIR requirement)
  • Employee Records: 3 years after employment ends (DOLE requirement)
  • Marketing Consents: Until withdrawn + 1 year
  • Technical Logs: 90 days maximum

8. Security Measures

  • End-to-end encryption for data transmission
  • Encrypted storage for sensitive business data
  • Multi-factor authentication options
  • Regular security audits and penetration testing
  • Role-based access controls
  • Automated backup and disaster recovery

9. Cookies and Tracking

We use cookies for:

  • Essential: Authentication and core functionality (no consent required)
  • Analytics: Usage statistics and performance monitoring (requires consent)
  • Marketing: Personalized advertising (requires explicit consent)

You can manage cookie preferences through our cookie banner or browser settings.

10. Data Breach Notification

In case of a personal data breach, we will:

  • Notify the National Privacy Commission within 72 hours
  • Inform affected users without undue delay
  • Provide clear information about the breach and remedial actions
  • Implement additional security measures to prevent recurrence

11. Children's Privacy

Our services are not intended for individuals under 18 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you become aware that a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us immediately.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any material changes by posting the new policy on this page and updating the "Last updated" date. For significant changes, we will provide additional notice via email.
